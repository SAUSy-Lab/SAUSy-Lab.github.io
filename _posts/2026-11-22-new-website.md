The lab has a new website. It's now served over HTTPS, and as you might have noticed, the design has been updated and modernized a little.

Everyone in the lab is now listed on the [Members](/members/) page, and most people have a profile with a short bio, their research keywords and links to their other work.

The posts from the old site are back too, at the same addresses they had before. You can filter them by category at the top of the [Posts](/posts/) page.

We plan to post here much more often: news from the lab, write-ups from seminars, and the occasional short analysis like the [King Street pieces](/2017/11/king-street-transit-pilot-project/) from 2017.
